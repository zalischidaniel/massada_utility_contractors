Think Smart, Dig Safe

Safety at Massada isn't just a policy; it's a daily operational standard. All employees are required to wear full protective gear (PPE) on every job site. Before any excavation, we rigorously verify APWA color codes to identify all underground utilities. Our crews follow strict trenching protocols, which include testing for atmospheric hazards in depths over four feet, keeping excavated soil at least two feet from trench edges, and mandating dedicated spotters for all excavator movements. Trenches are inspected at the start of every single shift. Together, we do our part to keep each other safe.
